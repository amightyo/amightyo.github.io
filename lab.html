INSIGHT Lab

INSIGHT Lab

Interdisciplinary Network for Science, Intelligence, Growth, Health & Technology

Turning data into insight. Turning insight into impact.

The INSIGHT Lab is an emerging interdisciplinary research initiative focused on advancing human-centered applications of data science, artificial intelligence, health analytics, education, and advanced quantitative methods.

Our Mission

The mission of the INSIGHT Lab is to advance interdisciplinary research that connects data, artificial intelligence, and quantitative methods with meaningful human and societal challenges.

The lab seeks to:

  • Conduct rigorous and impactful interdisciplinary research
  • Advance responsible and human-centered applications of artificial intelligence
  • Develop reproducible and transparent research workflows
  • Support doctoral students and emerging researchers
  • Promote collaboration across disciplines, institutions, and sectors
  • Translate analytical findings into actionable knowledge
  • Create opportunities for students to participate in research, publication, and scholarly dissemination

Research Pillars

Artificial Intelligence & Intelligent Systems

Research on predictive AI, generative AI, agentic AI, retrieval-augmented generation, human–AI collaboration, trust, explainability, responsible AI, and AI-supported decision-making.

Learning & Education Analytics

Research on AI literacy, learning analytics, student engagement, academic success, technology-enhanced learning, and responsible AI use in education.

Health & Wellness Analytics

Research applying data science and advanced analytics to sleep, physical activity, psychological wellness, population health, epidemiology, and health informatics.

Methods, Evidence & Open Science

Research advancing structural equation modeling, Bayesian methods, meta-analysis, bibliometrics, predictive modeling, reproducibility, and open science.

Current and Emerging Research Directions

The INSIGHT Lab is designed to support a portfolio of interconnected research projects rather than a single narrow research agenda.

Current and emerging areas of interest include:

Human–AI Decision-Making

Examining how individuals and organizations interact with artificial intelligence when making decisions, including questions related to trust, explainability, uncertainty, accountability, and decision quality.

AI Attitudes and Adoption

Investigating the individual, psychological, organizational, and contextual factors that shape attitudes toward artificial intelligence and influence AI adoption.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation and Emerging AI

Studying the evolution, research landscape, applications, and methodological development of retrieval-augmented generation and other emerging AI systems.

Health and Wellness Analytics

Using public health and population datasets to investigate relationships among sleep, physical activity, psychological wellness, metabolic risk, and other health outcomes.

Reproducible Bibliometric Research

Developing transparent and reusable workflows that integrate tools such as OpenAlex, R, Bibliometrix, VOSviewer, and computational text analysis.

Research Synthesis and Meta-Analysis

Synthesizing evidence across studies to better understand complex relationships in artificial intelligence, education, health, and organizational research.

Doctoral Research and Mentorship

A central goal of the INSIGHT Lab is to create opportunities for doctoral students and emerging scholars to develop as independent researchers.

Students affiliated with the lab may have opportunities to participate in:

  • Research design and theory development
  • Literature reviews and evidence synthesis
  • Data collection and secondary data analysis
  • Advanced statistical modeling
  • Machine learning and AI research
  • Bibliometric and meta-analytic studies
  • Reproducible research workflows
  • Conference presentations
  • Manuscript development
  • Open science and research dissemination

The lab will encourage doctoral researchers to move beyond completing individual studies toward developing broader programs of scholarship.

A Reproducible Research Philosophy

The INSIGHT Lab is committed to research practices that make scholarship more transparent, reliable, and reusable.

Where appropriate, projects will emphasize:

  • Pre-registration of research questions and analytical plans
  • Transparent documentation of research decisions
  • Version control using Git and GitHub
  • Reproducible analytical workflows
  • Open-source statistical tools
  • Public code repositories
  • Open Science Framework project documentation
  • Clear reporting of methods and limitations
  • Responsible and transparent use of artificial intelligence

Research workflows may incorporate tools such as R, Python, Quarto, GitHub, JASP, Jamovi, Stata, SAS, Power BI, OpenAlex, Bibliometrix, and VOSviewer, depending on the needs of each project.
