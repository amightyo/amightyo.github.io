Research Projects

From Questions to Evidence to Impact

My research projects reflect an interdisciplinary program of scholarship connecting artificial intelligence, data science, education, health analytics, research synthesis, and advanced quantitative methods.

Across these projects, I emphasize methodological rigor, reproducibility, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the translation of analytical findings into meaningful insights.

This page highlights selected active, ongoing, and emerging research projects. As projects develop, links to publications, conference presentations, open-source code, data, and reproducible research materials will be added.

Featured Projects

Artificial Intelligence

AI Literacy, Readiness, and Student Attitudes Toward Artificial Intelligence

Research Stream: AI, Data Science & Education

Methods: Structural Equation Modeling · Latent Variable Modeling

This research examines how AI literacy, readiness, confidence, anxiety, perceived relevance, satisfaction, and related factors contribute to students’ attitudes toward and intentions to use artificial intelligence.

The broader goal is to better understand the pathways through which students develop the knowledge, confidence, and motivation needed to engage effectively and responsibly with AI.

Key themes: AI literacy · Student readiness · AI confidence · Technology adoption · Higher education

Meta-Analysis

Personality Traits and Attitudes Toward Artificial Intelligence

Research Stream: AI, Data Science & Education

Status: Ongoing

Methods: Meta-Analysis · Research Synthesis

This meta-analysis examines the overall relationship between personality traits and attitudes toward artificial intelligence.

The project synthesizes empirical evidence across studies to determine the strength and consistency of associations between individual differences and how people perceive, accept, trust, or respond to AI.

The broader goal is to contribute to our understanding of the human factors that shape AI adoption and human–AI interaction.

Key themes: Personality · AI attitudes · Technology adoption · Human–AI interaction · Meta-analysis

Bibliometrics

Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Large Language Models

Research Stream: Science of Research & Emerging AI

Status: Ongoing

Methods: Bibliometric Analysis · Science Mapping · Computational Text Analysis

This project examines the development and intellectual structure of research on retrieval-augmented generation for large language models.

The study investigates publication trends, influential authors and institutions, international collaboration, citation structures, and emerging research themes.

A central methodological contribution is the development of a comprehensive and reproducible search and screening strategy using OpenAlex metadata rather than relying exclusively on the exact phrase “retrieval-augmented generation.”

Key themes: RAG · Large language models · Bibliometrics · OpenAlex · Emerging AI

Health Analytics

Sleep and Metabolic Health

Research Stream: Health & Wellness Analytics

Methods: Population Health Analytics · Regression · Bayesian Analysis

This research examines relationships among sleep characteristics and indicators of metabolic health using large-scale population health data.

The work considers whether sleep quality, sleep duration, and daytime functioning provide different insights into health risk and explores how analytical evidence can inform the prioritization of health indicators.

A broader goal of this research is to move beyond a singular focus on hours slept toward a multidimensional understanding of sleep and health.

Key themes: Sleep quality · Sleep duration · Metabolic health · Population health · Bayesian analysis

Open Science

Reproducible Bibliometric Research Workflow

Research Stream: Science of Research & Emerging AI

Status: In Development

Methods: Bibliometrics · Computational Research · Open Science

This project focuses on developing a robust and reusable workflow for conducting bibliometric research.

The workflow integrates data retrieval, screening, cleaning, analysis, visualization, documentation, and reproducibility using tools such as OpenAlex, R, Bibliometrix, VOSviewer, GitHub, and Quarto.

The long-term goal is to develop a transparent methodological framework that can support future bibliometric studies and potentially serve as the foundation for a methodological publication.

Key themes: Reproducible research · Bibliometrics · Open science · GitHub · Research workflows

Additional Research Directions

Physical Activity, Sleep, and Psychological Wellness

Research Stream: Health & Wellness Analytics

Methods: Structural Equation Modeling · Latent Variable Modeling

This project examines the relationships among physical activity, sleep, and psychological wellness using population-level health data.

The research considers whether physical activity and sleep operate through interconnected or parallel protective mechanisms and explores the use of latent constructs to represent multidimensional psychological wellness.

Key themes: Physical activity · Sleep · Psychological wellness · Health behavior · Structural equation modeling

Complementary Health Approaches and Health Behaviors

Research Stream: Health & Wellness Analytics

Methods: Population Health Analytics · Survey Data Analysis

This research uses national health survey data to examine patterns associated with complementary health approaches such as yoga, meditation, massage, acupuncture, chiropractic care, and related practices.

The project explores relationships with health status, stress, chronic pain, healthcare access, physical activity, sleep, and other health behaviors.

Key themes: Complementary health · Wellness · Health behaviors · Population health · Survey analytics

Bayesian Modeling for Health and Epidemiological Decision-Making

Research Stream: Health & Wellness Analytics / Advanced Quantitative Methods

Methods: Bayesian Statistics · Bayesian Model Averaging

This research explores how Bayesian methods can support evidence-based reasoning in health and epidemiological research.

The work emphasizes posterior probabilities, model uncertainty, inclusion probabilities, and evidence-based comparison of competing explanations rather than relying exclusively on traditional significance testing.

Key themes: Bayesian inference · Epidemiology · Model uncertainty · Health analytics · Decision-making

Reproducible Meta-Analysis Workflow

Research Stream: Advanced Quantitative Methods / Science of Research

Status: Emerging

Methods: Meta-Analysis · Research Synthesis · Open Science

This project explores the development of a transparent and reusable workflow for conducting quantitative research synthesis.

The workflow is envisioned to support literature searching, screening, coding, effect-size calculation, statistical modeling, sensitivity analysis, visualization, and reproducible reporting.

The goal is to strengthen transparency and consistency across meta-analytic research projects.

Key themes: Meta-analysis · Evidence synthesis · Reproducibility · Open science · Research methods

Emerging AI Research

Agentic AI and the Future of Research

Research Stream: Science of Research & Emerging AI

Status: Emerging Research Direction

As artificial intelligence systems become increasingly capable of planning, reasoning across multiple steps, using tools, and adapting their actions based on feedback, new questions are emerging about the role of AI agents in scientific research.

This emerging research direction explores how agentic AI may influence the research lifecycle, including:

  • Literature discovery
  • Evidence synthesis
  • Hypothesis development
  • Research design
  • Data analysis
  • Reproducibility
  • Scientific communication
  • Human oversight and research governance

A central question motivating this work is:

How can human researchers and increasingly autonomous AI systems collaborate while preserving scientific rigor, transparency, accountability, and human judgment?

This area represents a longer-term direction for my research program and the INSIGHT Lab.

Project Development Philosophy

I view research projects as part of a broader and evolving scholarly program rather than as isolated studies.

Where appropriate, projects are developed through a reproducible lifecycle:

Research Question → Theory → Protocol → Data → Analysis → Evidence → Publication → Open Research Outputs

Projects may incorporate:

  • Pre-registration and research protocols
  • Systematic literature searches
  • Transparent inclusion and exclusion criteria
  • Reproducible data preparation
  • Version-controlled analytical code
  • Sensitivity and robustness analyses
  • Open Science Framework documentation
  • GitHub repositories
  • Quarto-based research reports
  • Peer-reviewed publications and conference presentations

The goal is to create research that can be understood, evaluated, reproduced, and extended by others.
