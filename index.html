About My Work

My scholarship spans data science, artificial intelligence, health analytics, learning analytics, and advanced quantitative research methods. I am particularly interested in work that connects rigorous analytical methods with meaningful human and organizational outcomes.

I also mentor doctoral researchers and emerging scholars in developing research that is theoretically grounded, methodologically sound, reproducible, and positioned to make a practical contribution.
