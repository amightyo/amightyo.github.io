Open & Reproducible Scholarship

As my research program develops, I am working toward making more research outputs openly accessible and reproducible.

Where appropriate, future publications and projects will connect to:

  • GitHub repositories
  • Open Science Framework projects
  • Pre-registrations
  • Research protocols
  • Reproducible analytical code
  • Open datasets
  • Preprints
  • Supplementary materials

This approach supports the broader research philosophy of the INSIGHT Lab: creating scholarship that is rigorous, transparent, reproducible, and capable of being extended by others.
